# Give unendorsed public Pods bounded trial exposure

A newly admitted public Pod does not need a Pod Endorsement to become locally discoverable: when its identity, Origin availability, signed metadata, and bounded Explore samples verify, a strong local semantic match may earn it a small clearly labeled Exploration opportunity. Per-Origin and per-Pod caps limit spam, while explicit User feedback and local blocks adjust future exposure without creating global reputation or allowing endorsement networks to become discovery gatekeepers.
